# 06 — Ergonomics Reference

> Values are **approximate adult ranges for first CAD sizing**, from general anthropometric knowledge. Before freezing the headband or cup geometry, check them against a published dataset (e.g., ANSUR II, or a study of the target population) and against a few real heads. Taiwanese and Southeast Asian users may sit toward the lower-middle of these ranges; keep the full range unless a target population is chosen.

## 1. Head and ear dimensions (≈5th-percentile female … 95th-percentile male)

| Measure | Range [mm] | Drives |
|---|---|---|
| Head breadth (max, side to side) | ≈ 140 – 165 | Cup-to-cup spacing at rest; clamping force |
| Bitragion breadth (ear to ear at the tragus) | ≈ 130 – 155 | Cup centre positions |
| Bitragion–crown arc (over the top, ear to ear) | ≈ 330 – 380 | Headband length + adjustment range |
| Tragion to vertex (vertical) | ≈ 120 – 145 | Headband height adjustment |
| Pinna (ear) length | ≈ 55 – 70 | Ear-pad inner opening length |
| Pinna breadth | ≈ 30 – 40 | Ear-pad inner opening width |
| Pinna protrusion from head | ≈ 15 – 25 | Cup depth: the ear must not touch the driver grille |

## 2. Design targets

| Parameter | Target | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Ear-pad inner opening | ≈ 75 × 48 mm target, oval, long axis vertical; ≥ 65 × 45 mm absolute minimum | The target covers ≈95th-percentile pinna (≈70 mm) plus clearance; the minimum fits median ears only |
| Pinna-to-driver clearance | ≥ 5 mm with the pad compressed → head skin to grille ≈ 20–30 mm | Pad thickness + baffle recess (file 02 §3) |
| Headband adjustment | ≥ 25 mm per side (≥ 50 mm total), detents every ≈ 5 mm | Covers the ≈ 50 mm arc range above |
| Cup swivel | Yaw ±15–20°, tilt (pitch) ±10–15° about the cup centre | Seal on different head shapes |
| Clamping force | 2.5–4.5 N measured at 150 mm cup spacing | Too low → poor seal and bass; too high → discomfort after ~30 min |
| Pad contact pressure | Spread over ≥ 25 cm² per pad (wide, soft pads) | Comfort over long sessions |
| Mass | ≤ 300 g total, centre of mass close to the cup axis | Headband cushion ≥ 25 mm wide |
| Control reach | POWER/MFB/thumbwheel on the rear or lower rim of the left cup, reachable by the left thumb | Keys distinguishable by touch |
| Boom mic | Mic capsule ≈ 20–30 mm from the mouth corner when deployed | Boom length and jack position (front-lower) |

## 3. Fit test plan (with prints)

- Make 3 printed headband/cup mock-ups or one adjustable rig.
- Try at least 3–5 different people, including the smallest and largest heads available.
- Record the slider position, comfort after 30 min, pad seal (listen for the bass drop-out) and ear contact.
